## Player

The audio player allows you to control the audio in your project.

<img class="img-fluid" src="/images/docs/player.png" />

The player elements shown above are:

### 1. Audio waveform

This displays the audio waveform of the loaded file. You can also click on the waveform to navigate to
a particular section of the audio.

### 2. Player controls

This includes the play/pause button, stop button, volume control, trackbar, and time information.

### 4. Audio elements

This includes the loop audio button, a button to show/hide the audio waveform, and a button to show/hide the osilloscope.

### 5. Oscilloscope

This will display the audio signal while your audio is playing.
