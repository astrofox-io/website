## Load audio

There are two ways to load audio in Asrofox.

### Load through the menu

Click on **Menu > File > Load Audio** and select your audio file.

### Drag and drop

Drag a drop a file from your system directly onto the stage.

## Supported audio

The supported audio types are `.aac`, `.flac`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, and `.wav`.
